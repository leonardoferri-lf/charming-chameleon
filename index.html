ROS 02 · What we run

A production day,
end to end.

  1. PREP

    Operations direction

    Budgets, crewing, logistics, risk. The unglamorous architecture that decides whether a shoot succeeds before the first slate. Built for big teams, big territories and clients who cannot afford surprises.

  2. SHOOT

    Executive production

    Creative ambition held to commercial reality. Directing worldwide teams on set and remotely, with the calm that comes from having run rooms of every size, on every kind of budget.

  3. POST

    AI-integrated workflows

    AI woven into the pipeline where it earns its place: previsualisation, transcript-driven edits, automated versioning and localisation, budget and resource intelligence. Faster cycles, same craft.

  4. WRAP

    Delivery & aftercare

    Every deliverable, every spec, every market: reconciled, archived and reported. The wrap book your procurement team will actually enjoy reading.

ROS 04 · The operator

Leo Ferri

Two decades running productions where the stakes were high and the excuses weren't accepted.

Leo has directed operations and executive-produced for corporates, pharmaceutical and financial institutions, automotive brands and broadcasters, leading big teams across continents, on budgets from indulgent to impossible. The constant: productions that land on time, on budget and on brief.

Today that experience extends into AI-integrated production workflows. Not as a gimmick, but as an operational advantage clients can measure in cycles, versions and cost.
